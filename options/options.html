QuickScore — Settings

General

This only refreshes the popup/sidebar when it is actually open on the screen. Live match results are updated in the background all the time - see Notification Check Interval below.

A match that started late last night, or one starting in the early hours tomorrow, still shows up under "Today" by default. Each of these adds an additional request per league every time you open Today's view - if your timezone rarely (or never) has matches like this, or you simply prefer less requests, uncheck either one.

Notifications

"All live matches" takes priority if checked. Otherwise, only the matches you’ve marked as favorites and/or pinned (whichever you check) will trigger notifications.

You have to turn on "the system popup" explicitly - it is off by default. In both cases The toolbar icon count is always shown.

This runs in the background at all times and is what keeps live match results up to date - not just notifications .

"Goals" doesn't apply to basketball or American football - those score too often for a popup per point to be useful, so you'll only ever get a popup for their start/end, never for individual scores.

Leagues

Search and pick the leagues you want to follow. Click the × on a chip to remove it.

Favorite Teams

Favorite teams always show at the top of the popup, no matter which sport or league is selected.

Backup

Export everything (settings, favorite teams, leagues, pinned/muted matches) to a JSON file, or restore it on another browser/profile.
