# QuickCard

* **Install:** [Firefox Add-ons](https://addons.mozilla.org/en-US/firefox/addon/quickscore/)


Multi-sport live scores for football, basketball (NBA), American football (NFL), baseball (MLB), and hockey (NHL), all from one toolbar icon instead of five different sites.

Open the popup and you’ll see today’s games across 90+ competitions, including the World Cup, Champions League, Premier League, La Liga, Serie A, Bundesliga, NBA, NFL, MLB, NHL, and plenty more. Use the sport switcher at the top to focus on one sport, and pick exactly which leagues you care about in Settings.

**Follow what matters** 

* Favorite a team once, and its matches rise to the top, whether it’s a league game, a cup tie, or a continental night.
* Pin a single match for the day without favoriting the whole club.
* Mute notifications for one match only if that game stopped being interesting, while still getting alerts for everything else.

**Match details without leaving the popup**

Tap the info icon on a live or finished game:
* **Football:** goals, cards, substitutions; lineups with subs marked; stats like possession, shots, passes, corners, etc.
* **NBA & NHL:** scoring by period/quarter, the fouls and penalties that matter, and a full team comparison.
* **NFL & MLB:** scoring plays with the clock, plus a full team stat line.

**Notifications on your terms**

Choose what you get alerted for (kick-off, goals/scores, full time) and who it applies to: all live matches, favorites, pinned games, or any mix. Prefer a quiet badge on the toolbar, or full system pop-ups. Scores keep updating in the background even when the popup is closed.

**Small things that help day to day** 

* Games around midnight don’t fall through the cracks. A match still going at 1am, or one starting just before midnight, can stay under Today. You can turn either edge off in Settings.
* Jump to any date with the calendar, step day by day, or jump back to today in one tap.
* Export your leagues, favorites, and pinned/muted matches, then restore them on another computer or profile.
* Open it in the sidebar for a full-height view that stays put while you browse.
* Switch between light and dark themes whenever you want.

Match data comes from ESPN’s public feeds. Free, unofficial, and not affiliated with or endorsed by ESPN or any league.
